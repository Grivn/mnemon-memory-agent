\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

\sys{} divides the work of memory between two systems: a slow System~2, an LLM that words a few searches, names what a reply needs and composes the answer, and a fast System~1, a decision model that answers many small, explicit questions about the records those searches find.
Slow work that no reply can wait for, indexing each record once for the evidence no question points to, runs in the background, and the index points back to the raw records instead of replacing them.
With a small answering model, \sys{} is the most accurate system under a common re-evaluation protocol on LoCoMo and second on \lme, from under 4k tokens of context per question and with the lowest effective cost index on LoCoMo; with a reasoning reader it is level with the best published results on \lme; and its cost per question stays nearly flat from a hundred thousand to ten million tokens of history.
Most of the read-time work of memory is System~1 work, which a model built for it does better, faster and cheaper than a general LLM, and raw records leave room for better System~2 readers.
Code, prompts and analysis scripts are released with dsh-mnemon~\citep{mnemon2026code}.
