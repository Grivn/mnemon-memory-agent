\section{Implementation}
\label{sec:impl}

\sys{} runs as a \emph{replica}: a second DSH instance with the same Sources, its own Strategy and a control loop that calls \jev.
At each user message the replica runs one job and publishes its View into the main instance.
Upstream DSH is unmodified, and apart from 115 changed lines of dsh-mnemon the replica consists of new plugins~\citep{mnemon2026code}.

\paragraph{Journal.}
Records are chunks of at most about 600 characters, embedded locally with nomic-embed-text~\citep{nussbaum2024nomic}; a \lme{} history of about 106k tokens becomes roughly 1,000 records, written in under a second.
For BEAM's ten-million-token tier, the journal holds up to 200,000 records per conversation and keeps its search index between searches.

\paragraph{Consolidation.}
Background jobs read the journal from a watermark, in batches of about 10,000 characters that hold user messages whole and the first 240 characters of each assistant message, about 15\% of a chat's text.
DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash without thinking~\citep{deepseekflash2026} folds each batch into the index.
For very long memories a fold sees at most 120 topics, 240 values and 60 instructions, half chosen by the words they share with the batch and half by recency.
Index items are embedded as they are written, so that no View waits for them.

\paragraph{Models.}
System~2 is an LLM: the planner and the answering model are the same LLM, gpt-4.1-mini or DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash (\cref{sec:setup}), and the background folds use DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash without thinking.
System~1 is \jev{} 1.13, which judges up to 40 records per call and answers every question about them in that call; 24 records with two questions each take about 0.34\,s and cost \$0.00044.
